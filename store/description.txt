Can't decide what to watch? Let Crunchyroll pick for you.

Crunchyroll Random Anime adds a "Random Anime" button to the Crunchyroll header, right next to the search button. One click opens a random series from the whole Crunchyroll catalogue, so you discover something new instantly.

FEATURES
• Random Anime button built into the Crunchyroll navigation bar
• Toolbar popup with a "Surprise me" button that works from any tab
• Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+R (customizable at chrome://extensions/shortcuts)
• Follows your Crunchyroll language (French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, and more)
• Options: icon-only button, open random anime in a new tab
• English and French interface
• Lightweight: no tracking, no analytics, no data collected

HOW IT WORKS
The extension only runs on crunchyroll.com. It adds the button to the page and picks a series at random from Crunchyroll's public series list (its sitemap), which is cached in your browser for 3 days. Your settings are stored in your browser and never leave it.

Open source: https://github.com/noahbds/crunchyroll-random-anime
